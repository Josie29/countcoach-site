Support

Card Counter Coach is an offline blackjack trainer for learning Hi-Lo card counting and basic strategy. It does not use accounts, so there's nothing to sign in to and no data syncs between devices.

Having a problem?

Email 01josie@gmail.com with:

  • Your device model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • What you were doing when the issue happened
  • A screenshot, if it's a visual issue

Resetting progress

Deleting and reinstalling the app clears all local progress, since nothing is stored outside the device.

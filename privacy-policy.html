Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Card Counter Coach does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

  • The app makes no network requests. There are no servers involved in using Card Counter Coach.
  • There are no accounts, sign-in, or user profiles.
  • There is no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising software (SDKs) in the app.
  • All game progress and settings are stored only on your device, using Apple's local storage (@AppStorage). Deleting the app deletes this data.

If a future version of Card Counter Coach adds any data collection, analytics, or third-party software, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the App Store "What's New" notes.
